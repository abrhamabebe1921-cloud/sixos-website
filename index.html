About SIXOS

Bridging the Gap in Global Remote Operations

SIXOS is a B2B managed services company providing professionally managed remote teams for growing businesses worldwide.

Neither an Unwieldy Legacy BPO nor a Fragile Freelancer Marketplace.

Traditional large BPO providers are built for giant corporations — burdened with slow communication, rigid contracts, and depersonalized service. On the other end, hiring individual freelancers often results in fragmented accountability, inconsistent quality, and zero management supervision.

SIXOS creates the optimal middle ground: high-caliber remote talent backed by structured daily management, tailored onboarding, continuous training, rigorous quality control, and transparent operational reporting.

How It Works

A Clear, Guided Path to Deployment

From initial requirements to live operations, our structured process ensures zero disruption to your daily business.

01
Phase One

UNDERSTAND

We deep dive into your business model, customer touchpoints, current ticket volume or admin workflows, shift coverage requirements, and specific toolsets (e.g. Zendesk, Gorgias, Shopify, Slack, Asana).

02
Phase Two

BUILD

We architect your customized support structure: selecting candidate profiles, establishing SLA benchmarks, defining escalation paths, and configuring daily operational communications.

03
Phase Three

TRAIN

Selected team members complete intensive onboarding focused directly on your SOPs, brand voice, and response protocols, followed by mock ticket evaluations before taking live work.

04
FINAL STEP — YOU'RE LIVE

DELIVER

Your team goes live with active management supervision. Leads monitor queues, verify ticket resolution quality, and provide instant coaching to ensure steady performance from day one.

Security & Protection

A Protected Operational Ecosystem

We treat your client data, business accounts, and internal communications with strict procedural rigor.

Confidentiality Agreements
Binding non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) signed with every team member prior to onboarding on your account.
Strict Access Controls
Least-privilege access principles, secure password managers, and role-based permissions to ensure agents only access what they need.
Employee Security Training
Mandatory training covering phishing prevention, data protection, credential safety, and safe browsing hygiene.
Secure Account Practices
Enforcement of two-factor authentication (2FA/MFA) across all client tools, business portals, and internal communication channels.
Client-Specific Data Handling Protocols
Every business has unique operational guidelines. We configure our workflows, browser sandboxes, and VPN requirements according to your specific industry standards, contracts, and internal compliance policies.
Security and governance practices are adapted strictly in coordination with client infrastructure, contracts, and specific data protection guidelines.
